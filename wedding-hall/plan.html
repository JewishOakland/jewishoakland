How it works

From enquiry to event

  1. I

    Send an enquiry

    Tell us your preferred date, your event and your guest count, and confirm you have read the rules, using the form below.

  2. II

    The office replies

    We check the date, including Shabbat and Yom Tov timing, and send you a quote.

  3. III

    Confirm your date

    Your date is reserved once the rental agreement is signed and the reservation payment is received.

  4. IV

    Celebrate

    We send building access details before the event. Afterwards, the hall is inspected and your deposit returned.

What to tell us

What we'll ask you

  • Your preferred date and the hours you need, including setup and cleanup
  • The type of event
  • Your expected guest count
  • Your food and kitchen needs
  • Any equipment or special arrangements, such as music or outside vendors

The form below asks for the essentials. The office will follow up on the rest.

Rental rules

Catering, rental and cleanup

Catering and kitchen

The hall keeps kosher standards. All food brought into the hall must meet Chabad of Oakland's requirements, and all caterers and food arrangements are approved in advance.

Kitchen use is limited to approved refrigeration, reheating and warming. Full food preparation, unapproved cooking and outside cooking appliances are not permitted without written permission. Please leave the kitchen clean.

Deposit and payment

  • A 50% reservation payment secures your date.
  • The remaining balance is due 14 days before the event.
  • For bookings made within 14 days of the event, the full rental fee and deposit are due at booking.
  • A $500 refundable security deposit is required. Any deductions are itemized, and the balance is returned within 7 to 10 business days after the event and inspection.

Cancellation

  • More than 30 days before: the reservation payment is kept, and other rental payments are refunded.
  • 15 to 30 days before: 50% of the total rental fee is kept.
  • 14 days or fewer before: the full rental fee is due.
  • If Chabad of Oakland cancels, rental payments are refunded.

In exceptional circumstances, a date transfer or partial refund may be considered.

Setup and cleanup

You are responsible for all setup and cleanup, and both happen within your reserved period.

Before the event

  • Arrange the tables and chairs as needed
  • Bring all decorations, linens and serving supplies
  • Keep exits, hallways and accessible routes clear

After the event

  • Return tables and chairs to their original layout
  • Remove all food, drinks, decorations and belongings
  • Bag trash and use the designated bins
  • Wipe tables and clean any kitchen equipment and surfaces used

Cleanup must be finished before your reserved period ends. Extra time must be approved and paid for in advance.

Decorations and house rules

Signs, posters and banners may not be taped, pinned, nailed or stuck to the walls. Use freestanding displays or approved stands.

  • No smoking or vaping inside the hall
  • No open flames, including candles
  • No glitter or confetti
  • No nails, screws or strong adhesives on walls
  • Amplified music, alcohol and outside vendors must be disclosed and approved in advance

Community pricing

Reduced rates may be considered for Chabad-sponsored programs, recurring community programs, families experiencing financial hardship and other approved community events. Discounts must be agreed before the rental agreement is finalized.
