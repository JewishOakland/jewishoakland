The Space

The hall

A warm, light-filled room on Lake Merritt for weddings and family celebrations.

The room

One open, elegant room

Polished wood floors, exposed timber beams and a row of chandeliers give the hall its warmth. Tall windows run along one side and bring in daylight, and a bar with arched display cabinets stands beside them.

You have the hall to yourself for your whole reserved period, including setup and cleanup.

Layout and capacity

Up to about 160 guests

Tables and chairs can be set as long dinner tables, round tables or a buffet with seating.

Your approved guest count is confirmed for your layout, fire-safety rules and accessible routes, so not every setup will seat the full number. Tell us your expected count when you ask about a date.

Included

What's included

  • Exclusive use of the hall during your reserved period
  • The hall's existing tables and chairs
  • Standard lighting and electricity
  • Restroom access
  • Trash and recycling facilities

Not included: event staff, catering, table linens, dishes, serving supplies, decorations or cleaning services.

Facilities

Available facilities

  • Kitchen access for approved refrigeration, reheating and warming (optional, requested in advance)
  • Sound system and microphone, if available (optional)
  • Bar area with display cabinets

The hall keeps kosher standards, so all food and caterers are approved in advance. Fees for optional facilities are on the Plan Your Event page.

Accessibility

Accessibility

Every layout keeps accessible routes clear, and your guest count is confirmed with them in mind.

If you or your guests have accessibility needs, please tell the office when you ask about a date, or call 510.545.6770. We'll talk you through the route into the hall and the facilities before you book.

Parking and arrival

Getting here

The hall is at 3014 Lakeshore Avenue, Oakland, CA 94610, in the Grand Lake neighborhood beside Lake Merritt.

Parking. There is street parking nearby, and a parking lot diagonally across the street, under the I-580 freeway. The hall takes no responsibility for parking, for parked vehicles or for anything left in them.

Building access details are sent to you before your event. For questions about the entrance, call the office at 510.545.6770.
