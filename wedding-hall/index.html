Your reserved period includes setup, your event and cleanup. Cleanup must be finished and the hall vacated by the end of that time. Shabbat and Jewish holiday use requires advance approval and separate scheduling.

What's included

  • Exclusive use of the hall during your reserved period
  • Existing tables and chairs
  • Standard lighting and electricity
  • Restroom access
  • Trash and recycling facilities

Not included: event staff, catering, table linens, dishes, serving supplies, decorations or cleaning services. You arrange the tables and chairs and return them to their original layout.

Deposit & payment

  • A 50% reservation payment secures your date.
  • The remaining balance is due 14 days before the event.
  • Bookings made within 14 days: the full rental fee and deposit are due at booking.
  • A $500 refundable security deposit is required for each rental. Any deductions are itemized, and the balance is returned within 7 to 10 business days after the event and inspection.
  • A date is confirmed once the signed rental agreement and reservation payment are received.

Cancellation

  • More than 30 days before: the reservation payment is kept, and other rental payments are refunded.
  • 15 to 30 days before: 50% of the total rental fee is kept.
  • 14 days or fewer before: the full rental fee is due.
  • If Chabad of Oakland cancels, rental payments are refunded.
  • In exceptional circumstances, a date transfer or partial refund may be considered.

Cancellation terms are shared before any payment and follow California law.

Kitchen & kosher standards

Chabad of Oakland maintains kosher standards. All food brought into the hall must meet its requirements, and all caterers and food arrangements must be approved in advance.

Kitchen use is limited to approved refrigeration, reheating and warming. Full food preparation and unauthorized cooking are not permitted.

Decorations & house rules

Signs, posters and banners may not be taped, pinned, nailed or stuck to walls. Use freestanding displays or approved display stands.

  • No smoking or vaping inside the hall
  • No open flames, including candles
  • No glitter or confetti, and no illegal substances
  • No nails, screws or strong adhesives on walls
  • Amplified music, alcohol, outside vendors and other special arrangements must be disclosed and approved in advance

Guests & capacity

The hall holds approximately 160 guests. Your guest count is confirmed against your layout, fire-safety rules and accessible routes, so please share your expected number when you enquire.

Community pricing

Chabad of Oakland aims to keep the hall accessible to the Jewish community. Reduced rates may be considered for Chabad-sponsored programs, recurring community programs, families experiencing financial hardship and other approved community events.

Discounts are discretionary and must be agreed before the rental agreement is finalized.
