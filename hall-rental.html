How it works

Booking is simple

  1. Pick a date

    Tell us the date you have in mind, or a few options. We'll check availability with you.

  2. Tell us about your event

    Share your guest count, the setup you need and any questions. We'll put together a quote.

  3. Confirm and celebrate

    Once the details and agreement are set, your date is held and we'll help you get ready.
